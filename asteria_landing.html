ASTERIA Scientific Specification

Transit Photometry & Machine Learning Architecture

ASTERIA implements an end-to-end scientific pipeline designed to ingest NASA Transiting Exoplanet Survey Satellite (TESS) data, eliminate instrument artifacts, and accurately score planetary transit candidates.

01 Box Least Squares (BLS)

Evaluates periodic step-function transits over trial frequency grids, measuring transit duration, period, epoch, and transit depth $\delta = (R_p / R_*)^2$.

02 Feature Engineering

Computes signal-to-noise ratio (SNR), transit count, secondary eclipse depth, and odd-even transit depth differentials to immediately discard eclipsing binaries.

03 Random Forest / XGBoost

Trained on verified TESS Objects of Interest (TOIs) to assign a calibrated probability score separating confirmed candidates from stellar flares.

04 SHAP Interpretability

Provides game-theoretic Shapley attribution for every prediction, delivering audit-grade evidence for follow-up space telescope observations.
